# Demo data

Everything in this folder is fabricated. No real person, photo, name or registration number is used.

- `data/` and the scene images were generated by script. Names are "Demo Student NN" with registration numbers 9001 to 9024.
- `avatars-src/` holds 24 portraits: samples from the SFHQ (Synthetic Faces High Quality) set (Kaggle user SelfishGene), StyleGAN2-generated faces that depict nobody. Kaggle lists the dataset as CC0-1.0 (checked in its metadata). Source: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/selfishgene/synthetic-faces-high-quality-sfhq-part-1 . Each is the original resized to 256 px.
- `apply_faces.py` writes those portraits into `img/` (as people avatars and as the faces in each photo, at the boxes listed in `data/photos.json`).
- `check_demo.py` checks that the data files agree with each other.

Synthetic faces were chosen on purpose: scraped photos of real people carry privacy risk even when the copyright is free, and several other synthetic-face sets are non-commercial only.
