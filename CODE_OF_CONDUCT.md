# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). The short version:

- Be welcoming and respectful. Assume good intent and give people room to learn.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks, in issues, pull requests, reviews or anywhere else the project is represented.
- Give and take feedback gracefully. Critique the code, not the person.
- Respect privacy. Never share other people's personal information, and never share photos or data of students.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, or block accounts that break these rules. To report a problem, email **codewithpom@gmail.com**. Reports are handled privately.
